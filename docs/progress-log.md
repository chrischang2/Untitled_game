# Willow Lake: progress log

A running summary of what's been built, session by session. Newest session at the bottom. For how things work, see [companion-genai-plan.md](companion-genai-plan.md); for controls, see the [README](../README.md).

## What the game is now

Willow Lake is a cozy low-poly fishing game in Unity 6 for learning Mandarin by voice. Everything runs locally on this PC, and all assets are free.

- **Mei (美)** is your friend and Mandarin tutor, and your only source of Chinese words. She explains in English as much as her level allows (Esc → Mei's English: Beginner / Intermediate / Immersion).
- **The market** has four shopkeepers who speak only Mandarin:
  - 老王 Old Wang (tackle)
  - 陈阿姨 Auntie Chen (buys your fish)
  - 李师傅 Master Li (furniture)
  - 小林 Xiao Lin (pet shop)
- **Tangyuan (汤圆)** is your cat. She gets hungry, follows you when she's happy, and uses the bowl, bed and toys you buy.
- **Dialogue is Chinese only; menus are English.** Pinyin is generated by the game from CC-CEDICT, never by the AI.
- **The AI stack:**
  - Qwen3.5-4B, run by llama-server, does the thinking.
  - SenseVoice, pinned to Mandarin, does speech recognition.
  - Kokoro, Matcha and Piper do the voices, through sherpa-onnx.
- **Testing:** `Builds/WillowLake/WillowLake.exe -selftest` plays the whole game with synthesised voices and writes `Captures/selftest/report.txt`.

Built:
- **Qwen3-ASR, tested.** I ran SenseVoice and Qwen3-ASR 0.6B (int8) side by side on your 28 recordings from the first play session:
  - **On your Mandarin, Qwen3 was worse.** Plain, it heard 喂汤圆 as 为唐人, 我想买小鱼干 as 果香味、香油干, and one clip in Cantonese.
  - **With the game's words as hotwords** it fixed those, but it sometimes "hallucinated" the hotword list itself (6 of 28 clips with a long list), swapped 买/卖, and took 1–8 s per line (SenseVoice: 0.1 s).
  - **On English and mixed lines it's clearly better:** "What does chongwu dian sell?" against SenseVoice's "WHAT DOES 冲电 SELL".
  - **So the game now uses both** (Esc → Speech recognition, default **Auto**). SenseVoice hears everything, and lines with English in them are re-heard by Qwen3 (no hotwords; runaway output is caught and discarded). The other settings are "SenseVoice only" and "Qwen3-ASR (beta)" for everything. The chat log records which one was used.
  - The setup script downloads the model (837 MB).
- **Longer days.** The default is now 120 real seconds per game hour (was 60). **The waking day (6am–2am) lasts 40 minutes** (was 20). Esc → Day length offers 30 / 40 / 60 minutes, and existing settings were moved to the new default.
- **Energy** (`Progression/Energy.cs`, bar under the money):
  - **What it costs:** casting costs 1.5. Reeling drains continuously by weight: about 0.5/s for a sardine, about 7/s for a 150 kg tuna.
  - **Cutting the line:** press **E** while reeling to cut it and save energy.
  - **Max energy** comes from your bed (sleeping mat 100, single bed 140, double bed 180, big soft bed 230; Master Li sells them) plus 10 per home comfort level. Comfort comes from placed furniture (sofa 3, lamp/rug/bookcase/radio 2, others 1); levels are at 3, 6, 10, 15 and 20 comfort points.
- **Days end** (`Home/SleepSystem.cs`):
  - **Sleeping:** use the bed in the house from 6pm and you wake at 6am with full energy.
  - **Passing out:** at 2am, or at zero energy, you pass out. Your bag keeps only its 3 most valuable slots, and you wake at **10am in the house** with 75% energy. The boat is towed back to the dock.
  - **Warnings** come at midnight and when energy is low.
- **Bag slots** (`Economy/Inventory.cs`):
  - Everything you carry takes a slot: bait, cat food, gifts, unplaced furniture, unread books, and fish. Fish stack 20 of a kind per slot; other things 99.
